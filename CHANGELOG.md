# Updates

## October 25, 2026

1. **Price Order** — **On display** is one row of cards with the photo, zoom, name, price, delete, curtain, price switch, and arrows to rearrange. The last card is **Add item** and **Load saved item**. Cards slide into place on the desk and the big screen, and photos stay square. **Reveal order** sorts cheapest to most expensive without changing prices or curtains, then turns off until the order is wrong again.
1. **Elimination Trivia** — If nobody survives a question, **Try this round again** puts those players back in. Enter a different question (or use **Load saved question**), then open voting.
1. **Elimination Trivia** — Next to the current question, **Load saved question** opens saved questions. **Use now** puts one straight into the current question (while on standby).

1. **Friendly Feud** — **Reveal** is a green outline and **Hide** is solid amber so you can tell at a glance which answers are already on screen.
1. **Friendly Feud** — **Show question** / **Hide question** is a larger labeled button next to the question picker. **Show** is outline; **Hide** is solid.
1. **Message Board** — **Show player QR** / **Hide player QR** replaces the message with a large player scan code under the logo (no blur). **Show** is outline; **Hide** is solid.
1. **Word Up** — **Strikes** toggle (next to Letter legend) turns the big-screen X on or off for wrong letters. Letters still mark as guessed either way.
1. **Word Up** — **Save to bank** / **Update in bank** puts the current phrase, topic, and board zoom into Phrases and topics. A toast confirms the save. Each saved phrase shows its zoom in that list.
1. **Live Drawer** — On narrower operator screens, the color count **+** / **−** buttons stay fully visible instead of getting cut off.

1. **Elimination Trivia** — Configurable countdown with **30s** / **45s** / **1m** presets or a custom time. Start, pause, reset, and **Show** / **Hide** it on the big screen (top left).

1. **Elimination Trivia** — The big screen stays on standby while you prep a question. The question appears when you **Open voting**.
1. **Elimination Trivia** — **Lock voting** pauses the countdown at the time left instead of jumping to zero.

1. **Elimination Trivia** — After you pick who survives, phones update right away to won or out.
1. **Elimination Trivia** — When voting is locked, phones keep the answer buttons on screen (disabled) with **Voting is closed**.
