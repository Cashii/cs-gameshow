# Updates

## September 3, 2026

1. **Price Order** — Each item can sit behind a curtain. New photos start covered; open them one at a time or reveal / close all from the top bar. Item names stay under the curtain too, wrap on longer text, and unplaced shelf items are larger.
1. **Question Time** — Add and manage more than two teams (up to eight).
1. **Take It or Leave It** — Cases hold green or red cards instead of money. Guests pick a case on their phones (many can share a case); when that case is opened they see keep-playing or eliminated. While the game is live, the operator always sees green/red answers and live vote counts per case. The big screen no longer shows the game title.
1. **Elimination Trivia** — Stage upcoming questions in a queue, and review past rounds (question, split, remaining) under History.
1. **Poll** — Past polls are kept so you can review earlier questions, tallies, and vote logs after you open a new one or clear.
1. **Derby Race** — Guests pick a toy or horse on their phones before the race. Operator sees live vote tallies on each racer and in the top bar; voting locks when the race starts.
1. **Pictionary** — New game page. Type the word to show or cover, flash hints by revealing letters of the word, and run a timer.
1. **Branding** — Jack'd Up logo now appears on operator, spectator, player screens and colors have been updated on spectator screens to match the logo color palette
1. **Uploaded photos** — Photos fit the square instead of crop-fit so photos uploaded that aren't perfect square don't zoom in on the base size. This impacts price guesser and price order
1. **Live Drawer** — The page scroll bug is fixed.
1. **Strikes** — Friendly Feud and Wheel of Riches still flash the X, with no buzzer sound effect.
