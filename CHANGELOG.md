# Updates

## September 22, 2026

1. **Price Order** — Ranking slots grow to fill available space under the shelf; item curtains cover the photo and the item name; rank numbers are larger; photos and price tags stay matched without clipping.
1. **Poll** — Toggle **Percentages shown / hidden** to hide live percentage bars on the big screen and phones (operator tally still updates). For quiz-style polls, mark the correct choice, then Reveal correct answer.
1. **Derby Race** — Wonderbar theme no longer shows the Wonder BAR logo lockup (title only). After a race finishes, winner picks are locked and a clear message points you to Race again or Reset.
1. **Elimination Trivia** — Upcoming answer fields accept spaces while typing. Question 1 question and answers stay saved when you switch screens (saved when you leave the field).
1. **Friendly Feud** — Team names sit beside the board in larger type so they’re easier to read. Under Award to, choose **No one** to reveal an answer without giving points. Long answers fit better in the answer tiles.
1. **Wheel of Riches** — The topic banner shows the full topic, wraps when needed, and spans nearly the full screen width with side margins.
1. **Pictionary** — Hint letters and the full word wrap only between words, so phrases like “Tall Safari Animal” never split a word across lines.

## September 3, 2026

1. **Branding** — Jack'd Up logo now appears on operator, spectator, player screens and colors have been updated on the app to match the logo color palette.
1. **Updates** — This page now exists to inform users of changes. Each update shows its game icon, a bold title, and description of the update.
1. **Strikes** — Friendly Feud and Wheel of Riches flash no longer emits a buzzer sound effect when the X strike appears.
1. **Price Order** — Each item sits can now sit behind a curtain. New items start covered; open them one at a time or reveal / close all from the top bar.
1. **Question Time** — Add and manage more than two teams (up to eight). Title is now configurable.
1. **Elimination Trivia** — Preparing multiple questions in a queue is possible and review past rounds (question, split, remaining) under History. Each question can have two to five answers.
1. **Poll** — Past polls are kept so you can review earlier questions, tallies, and vote logs after you open a new one or clear.
1. **Derby Race** — Guests pick a toy or horse on their phones before the race. A large QR code overlay appears during derby betting phase so participants can join. Operator sees live vote tallies on each racer and in the top bar; betting locks when the race starts.
1. **Pictionary** — New game. Type the word to show or cover with a curtain, flash hints by revealing letters of the word, and run a configurable timer.
1. **Uploaded photos** — Photos fit the square instead of crop-fit so photos uploaded that aren't perfect squares don't appeared zoomed in at the base size.
1. **Live Drawer** — The page scroll bug is fixed. Tokens stay the same size whether the number is one, two, or three digits.
1. **Take It or Leave It** — Cases hold green or red cards instead of money. Players pick a case on their phones using player screen; A large QR code overlay appears on the spectator screen during setup and picking so participants can play. When a case is opened players can see if they can keep-playing or are eliminated. While the game is live, the operator always sees green/red answers and live vote counts per case. The big screen no longer shows the game title.
