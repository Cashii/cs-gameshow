# Question Time

Newlywed-style question round. Couples play in the room; the screens only assist. The operator puts a question on the projector, tracks two team scores, and runs a giant countdown.

There is no player phone board.

## Operator

1. Select **Question Time** in the left nav (under Price Order).
2. Put **Question Time** on the Spectator screen.
3. Type the question. It appears live on the projector.
4. Name the two teams and tap **+1** / **+5** / **+10** (or type a score) as they earn points.
5. Pick a timer length (presets or minutes/seconds), then **Start**. **Pause**, **Resume**, and **Reset clock** control the same countdown the audience sees.
6. **Clear question** blanks the board. **Reset scores** zeros both teams. **Reset game** clears the question, scores, and clock but keeps team names and the timer length.

## Spectator

Indigo night-sky board with a glass question card, two team scores, and a large ring countdown. The clock turns urgent in the last ten seconds and holds at `0:00` when time is up.
