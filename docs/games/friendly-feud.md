# Friendly Feud

Survey-style board game with team scores, strikes, and answer reveals.

## Operator controls

- Select round, reveal answers, add strikes
- Adjust team names and scores
- Toggle header, team scores, and answer point values on spectator

## Spectator

Shows the question, answer board (hidden/revealed), strike count, and team scores.

## Questions

**Save this question** stores the round on screen, including every answer and its points. **Save all questions** stores every round. **Load** puts that question on the current round and hides its answers. **Load all** replaces the board. Team names and scores stay.
