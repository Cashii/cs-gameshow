# Price Order

The operator loads up to five product photos. They appear together on the big screen. A player (in the room) calls an order from cheapest to most expensive. The operator rearranges those cards for the crowd, then reveals the order, which sorts itself from least to greatest price. Prices stay hidden or showing as the operator left them.

There is no player phone board.

## Operator

1. Select **Price Order** in the left nav.
2. Put **Price Order** on the Spectator screen.
3. **On display** is one row of cards. **Add item** on the last card starts a blank item, or **Load saved item** loads a saved photo, name, and price. Up to five. A new photo starts behind a closed curtain.
4. Each card has the photo (with zoom), name, price, delete, **Curtain closed** / **Curtain open**, and **Price hidden** / **Price showing**. The top bar can still reveal or close every curtain, and **Hide all prices** covers the prices again.
5. Use the left and right arrows on each card to rearrange them into the order the player calls. The cards slide into place on the desk and on the big screen.
6. When the player confirms, **Reveal order** slides the row from cheapest to most expensive, on the desk and on the big screen. Prices and curtains stay as you left them. The button turns off after that. It turns on again only if you change the order and that order is no longer cheapest to most expensive.
7. **Show Perfect order** or **Show Not quite** puts the result graphic on the spectator screen, based on the order before the sort. **Hide result** takes it down. Rearranging or editing a price clears that call so you can judge again.
8. **Reset game** removes everything.

## Spectator

- Photos sit in one row, in the order the operator set. There is no shelf and no empty slots.
- Curtains cover a photo and its name until opened. Price tags stay hidden until revealed.
- **Reveal order** slides the row into cheapest → most expensive. Price tags stay hidden until you show them.
- The operator can then show a **Perfect order** or **Not quite** graphic over a darkened mask.

## Saved items

The folder icon on the game actions bar, and **Load saved item** on the dashed tile, open the same gallery Price is Tight uses. **Save all items** stores each item on the board, with its photo, name, and price. Pick a photo to add that item, up to five. Resetting the live game does not delete saved photos.
