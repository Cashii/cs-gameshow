# Take It or Leave It

Cases hold green or red cards. Green means keep playing; red means eliminated. Guests pick a case on their phones. The operator opens cases one by one.

There is no money board and no banker.

## Operator

1. Select **Take It or Leave It** in the left nav.
2. Put it on the Spectator screen so phones follow.
3. In setup, set each case to **Green** or **Red** (add/remove cases, 3–16). Tap **Start picking**. Players are optional — you can start with nobody on phones yet.
4. Guests claim cases on `/player` (one pick each; several phones may share a case). On the operator screen you always see green/red and live **vote counts** per case. Then **Begin opening cases** (even if nobody has picked yet).
5. Click cases to reveal them on the big screen. Anyone who picked that case sees the same result on their phone.
6. **Reset Game** returns to setup and keeps the current green/red deck.

## Player (`/player`)

While Spectator is on Take It or Leave It (or Take It is the active operator game):

- **Picking:** choose any case (others can pick the same one).
- **Waiting:** your case is locked until the operator opens it.
- **Opened:** full-screen green (keep playing) or red (eliminated).

## Spectator

Centered case grid only. Opened cases show a green or red card. Claimed cases are marked until opened.
